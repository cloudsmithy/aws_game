Shield Advanced

Security groupGlobal serviceSubscriptionGlobal service

DDoS protection: Shield Standard is included free on every account and blocks common network-layer attacks. Shield Advanced is a paid subscription that adds application-layer protection, Shield Response Team (SRT) support, and credits for scaling costs during an attack.

Common uses

Large websites, games, and financial workloads that are sensitive to DDoS

Why use it

Withstands large DDoS attacks: an expert team responds around the clock, and you can request credits for the extra scaling costs incurred during an attack

Key concepts5 points

  1. Shield Standard: every customer gets it automatically for free; it protects against common network-layer and transport-layer DDoS attacks
  2. Shield Advanced: paid (a fixed monthly fee plus data transfer fees); it protects CloudFront, Route 53, ELB, EC2 Elastic IPs, and Global Accelerator
  3. Advanced includes the 24/7 Shield Response Team (SRT), lets you request credits for the extra scaling costs incurred during an attack, and shows attacks in more detail
  4. Together with WAF, it can automatically mitigate application-layer (layer 7) DDoS attacks
  5. Suits public-facing, high-traffic, business-critical workloads

3.To use the Shield response team (SRT) with Shield Advanced, which support plan do you need to subscribe to?
AnswerBusiness Support+ or Enterprise Support
Starting in 2027, the Developer and Business support plans are retired and replaced by Business Support+
4.A small company's website is deployed on Amazon Web Services, and it has not purchased any additional protection services. Which protection against common network-layer and transport-layer DDoS attacks does it get automatically?CLF-C02
AnswerShield Standard, at no additional charge
All customers are automatically protected by Shield Standard at no additional charge, which defends against the most common network-layer and transport-layer DDoS attacks. Shield Advanced requires a separate paid subscription; Macie and Artifact are unrelated to DDoS protection. Official docs ↗
5.A game company has suffered a large-scale DDoS attack. It wants help from the Amazon Web Services expert team during attacks, and protection against some of the cost spikes the attacks cause. Which service should it choose?SAA-C03
AnswerSubscribe to Shield Advanced
Shield Advanced provides Shield Response Team (SRT) support and cost protection against DDoS-related cost spikes. Shield Standard is free and takes effect automatically, but does not include either of these; GuardDuty is for threat detection and Inspector is for vulnerability scanning, so neither is a DDoS protection service.
