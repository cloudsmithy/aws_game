Route 53

Networking groupGlobal serviceBy usageGlobal service

Managed DNS: registers and resolves domain names, can fail over based on health checks, and routes users to different Regions by latency or geolocation. A global service with a 100% availability SLA.

Common uses

Domain name resolution, cross-Region disaster recovery failover

Why use it

DNS resolution and health checks together: if one side goes down, traffic switches to the other automatically

Key concepts5 points

  1. Managed DNS plus domain registration, with a 100% availability commitment in its service level agreement (SLA)
  2. Routing policies: simple, weighted, latency-based, failover, geolocation, geoproximity, and multivalue answer
  3. Health checks with failover routing: if the primary site goes down, users are switched to the backup site automatically
  4. Alias records can point directly to ELB, CloudFront, S3 static websites, and more, and these queries are free
  5. A private hosted zone resolves only within its associated VPCs
