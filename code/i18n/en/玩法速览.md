# Amazon Web Services User Group Cloud Tycoon · Quick Start (short version)

Read it in three minutes and you can start playing. For details see the Player Manual (玩法手册); for all rules and numbers see the Host Rulebook (主持人规则书).

> **Disclaimer**: Certain generative AI services from Amazon Web Services featured in this game are available only in Amazon Web Services Regions outside mainland China. The Amazon Web Services User Group presents them only to help you learn about cutting-edge technology and grow your business overseas. Cloud services in the Amazon Web Services China Regions are operated by Sinnet and NWCD; refer to the China Regions website for details. ## 1. The game in one paragraph

You run a company that is starting up on Amazon Web Services: buy services, and when other players land on your services they pay you rent; upgrade your services and complete architectures to collect more; survive production incidents and keep your bills under control. The last player who has not gone bankrupt wins, or, when the round limit is reached, players are ranked by valuation. The gameplay is inspired by classic property-trading board games.

## 2. Setting up

1. Pick the number of players (2–4), the mode and the win conditions, and click “Start game”. Not enough players? Tick “CPU”.
2. Each player is randomly dealt a role card: starting cash, ability, weakness and one signature skill. If “Choose from 3 roles” is ticked on the start screen, each player looks at 3 cards and picks one.
3. The default is Standard. For your first game, switch to **Beginner** and keep the default win conditions (Classic + 15-round limit, about 20 minutes).

## 3. One turn

1. Roll two dice and move forward; if you roll doubles, roll again.
2. Resolve the square you land on: a service nobody owns can be bought; pay rent on another player’s service; on your own service you can upgrade or add capacity; draw a card on card squares; pay money on bill squares.
3. Each time you pass Startup Credits, collect $200 in Credits and pay this lap’s running cost at the same time.
4. Landing on “Root Key Leak”, or rolling doubles three times in a row in the same turn, sends you straight to the **Maintenance Window**: on your next turn choose one, pay $50 to leave right away and roll the dice as normal, or skip a round and leave afterward (Traditional Enterprise Moving to the Cloud skips 2 rounds). If you walk onto the Maintenance Window square yourself, you also skip a round or pay $50.
5. You can quiz an opponent (only one of these per turn): pay $100 to quiz one opponent (once per game); if they answer correctly they keep the $100, and if they answer wrongly they pay you $200. Or quiz the whole table (once every 3 laps): each opponent gets one question, $50 each; a correct answer keeps the $50, a wrong answer pays you $100.
6. Once per lap you can pay to make an opponent draw a Health card: the price is 1.5× the cost of drawing a re:Invent card, and you can pick one opponent, choose one at random, or pay double to make every opponent draw one.

## 4. Making and spending money

The more expensive the service, the more upgrades and the more capacity, the more rent you collect; complete a whole color group and even services with no upgrades collect double. In Standard, Full Game and Study Mode, the first 2 rounds are a new-account protection period, and each rent is capped at $50.

Complete a real architecture (for example ELB → EC2 → RDS), and when another player lands on any one of its services, you collect for the whole set. Instance-type services cost running cost even when idle, while Serverless costs nothing when unused. Rent goes up a tier every 5 rounds, and late in the game a single good square can bankrupt someone.

## 5. Avoiding incidents

Drawing a Health Event card (AZ power outage, DDoS, certificate expiry…) gets you penalized. If you have built the matching high availability and security features, you are immune: for example, Multi-AZ survives an AZ power outage, Auto Scaling survives a traffic spike, and backups survive accidental deletion.

## 6. Short of cash and bankruptcy

- Remove upgrades or reduce capacity: refunds half. A service that can be stopped can be taken offline: you get half of the purchase price back as working cash, it must stay offline for at least 3 rounds before you can pay to bring it back online, and while it is offline another player who lands on it can pay the original price to take it away (Party Mode has no taking offline).
- Sell a service you don’t want back to Amazon Web Services as a whole: you get back 80% of what you actually paid.
- You go bankrupt only if selling everything still doesn’t cover the debt, and your services are split among creditors in proportion to what you owe each.

## 7. Signature skill

Each role has a comeback skill. When your **net worth falls below half of your starting cash**, click “Signature skill” to use it, **once per game**: the role’s own skill resolves first, then a “Strategic Acquisition” follows: you buy one service from the opponent with the highest net worth, at the sell-back price (the money goes to that opponent).

## 8. Modes and switches

| Option | In one line |
|---|---|
| Party Mode | Common services + mystery box squares, the most card drawing, 10 rounds |
| Beginner | Basic rules only, to get familiar |
| Standard | Adds tech debt and quarterly earnings, for counting the bills seriously |
| Full Game | Adds Actions, full trading (held cards and markers can be traded too), Savings Plans / RI |
| Study Mode | Required questions, mistake log, no round limit |
| Hell Mode | Everything turned up to the maximum, questions required |
| Rapid Deploy (switch) | Deploy points 0–12, spend points to use 12 skills, 12 points can DDoS an opponent |
| Reseller Mode (switch) | Seats can act as Resellers: buy wholesale at a discount, resell, and earn the running cost difference |
| Quiz While Playing (switch) | A correct answer halves rent and gives 20% off when buying a service |

## 9. Three tips

- Don’t spend all your cash: running costs are due every lap, and if your cash is too low to pay even once, you are forced to sell off assets.
- Build Multi-AZ first: it is the best-value first protection.
- Complete color groups and architectures first: it is the way to collect the most.
